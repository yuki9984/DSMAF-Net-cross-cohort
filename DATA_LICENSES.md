# Data provenance and permitted reuse

This release contains research-release identifiers, fixed study partitions, processed metadata, diagnostic reference labels, and study-generated individual model outputs. It does not contain private clinical records, free-text notes, passwords, correspondence, or author keys. Original research identifiers are not real patient names and must not be used to attempt reidentification.

* **PAD-UFES-20 v1**: Andre G. C. Pacheco and coauthors, DOI https://doi.org/10.17632/zr7vgbcyr2.1. Source data: **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Study partitions, encodings and exclusions are derived research materials; retain attribution and identify these changes.
* **DermatoNet-TelessaudeRS v2**: Camila Alves Dias and coauthors, DOI https://doi.org/10.17632/g5bj76p2z3.2. Source data: **CC BY 4.0**. The study excluded 18 images in eight conflicting-label duplicate groups and retained 1,701 cases; see the preserved provenance and case tables.
* **MILK10k**: MILK study team, DOI https://doi.org/10.34970/648456; dataset and per-image attribution/license fields: https://api.isic-archive.com/doi/milk10k/ . The official release marks the images **CC BY-NC**. Preserve the specific `copyright_license` and `attribution` values in the supplied case table; non-commercial use and attribution are required. The study selects 4,474 clinical images with distinct lesion IDs, maps diagnostic categories, and produces model predictions and response tables. Patient linkage is unavailable.

The combined saved-output package is supplied for non-commercial research verification with attribution; original source terms continue to apply to the corresponding records. This notice does not relicense third-party datasets or neural dependencies. Original photographs are obtained from their providers under their own terms. Numeric model parameters contain no third-party source code; the executable model still requires the separately obtained dependencies and their licenses described in DEPENDENCY_ACCESS.md.
